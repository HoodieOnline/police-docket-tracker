# Docket Tracker SA

A Flask prototype for a police-station case workflow based on `USECASES FOR POLICE STATION SYSTEM-0.docx`.

## Implemented use cases

- **Complainant:** creates an account, signs in, submits an incident statement and follows only their own case status/history.
- **Administration clerk:** captures or verifies a complaint and files the electronic or physical docket, including its shelf location and evidence list. Clerks do not assign detectives. Employee numbers are generated by the system; detective specialty/unit can be recorded at registration.
- **Station Commander:** uses the command dashboard to review filed dockets, assign or reassign an active station detective using specialty and current workload, and approve closure only after reviewing a detective's submitted investigation report and evidence.
- **Detective:** receives assignment notifications, confirms receipt of the docket, progresses the investigation, submits the report for Commander review and records docket hand-offs.
- **System:** stores statements and case-status history, scopes staff access to a station or assigned caseload, records docket movements, keeps closed docket records, and generates closed-case statistics for a selected date range.

New reports move from **Submitted** to **Registered** only after the clerk files the docket. The Station Commander assigns a detective from the dashboard; detectives progress assigned cases through investigation states and submit a report for review. A Station Commander approves closure with a recorded outcome and reason, and the complainant receives a status notification. Every docket movement records actor, origin, destination, date/time, movement type, reason and (when applicable) recipient acknowledgment.

## Case workflow

1. **Complainant submits a report.** A complainant registers an account (or signs in), enters the incident date, location, category and statement, and submits it to the selected station. The new case starts at **Submitted**. The complainant can see only their own cases, status history and notifications.
2. **Clerk reviews and files the docket.** The station clerk opens the submitted report, verifies its details, chooses electronic or physical format, records the shelf/storage location and lists evidence (enter `None` if there is no evidence to record). A physical docket also requires its printed serial number. Filing creates the docket and moves the case to **Registered**. The clerk does not assign detectives.
3. **Station Commander assigns an investigator.** The Commander dashboard lists registered dockets awaiting assignment. It shows docket format, physical serial, shelf location and recorded evidence. The Commander chooses an active detective at that station, using the detective's specialty and active-case count to guide the selection. Assignment moves the case to **Assigned**, records the docket hand-off, and notifies the detective.
4. **Detective receives and investigates.** The assigned detective confirms receipt of the physical or electronic docket; the confirmation is stored in the movement history. The detective updates the case through the available investigation statuses and records findings, evidence collected, actions taken and outstanding work in the required status-change reason. When the investigation report is ready, the detective selects **Awaiting Supervisor Review** to submit it to the Commander.
5. **Commander reviews and approves closure.** A case cannot be closed before it reaches **Awaiting Supervisor Review**. The Commander reviews the case statement, investigation updates, recorded evidence and docket history, confirms that review in the approval form, then records an outcome and reason. Approval changes the case and docket to **Closed**, retains their history, and queues a status notification for the complainant.
6. **Complainant follows the outcome.** The complainant signs in to view current status, status history and messages. Closure is reflected on the same case; the record is retained rather than deleted.

**Status path:** `Submitted` → `Registered` → `Assigned` → investigation statuses → `Awaiting Supervisor Review` → `Closed`. Clerks control filing, detectives control investigation updates, and only the Station Commander approves detective assignment and closure. Case details, status changes, assignments, notifications and docket movements are recorded for accountability.

The SQLite schema includes users, complainants, employees, roles, stations, cases, dockets, detective assignments, docket movements, notifications, case-status history, and audit events. Existing local databases are extended in place on startup; no existing case rows are deleted.

### Notifications and SMS

In-app messages and SMS notification records are written to the notification outbox. **No SMS is actually sent by this prototype.** SMS records remain marked `Queued - SMS gateway not configured`; connect and verify an approved SMS provider before relying on delivery or describing a message as sent.

### Security and operational limits

Passwords are stored with salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256; existing SHA-256 demo hashes are upgraded on successful login. Demo users remain for local evaluation and must be removed or changed before deployment. Set `DOCKET_TRACKER_SECRET_KEY` to a strong random value and use a production database, HTTPS, CSRF protection, access review, backups and an approved SMS gateway before real use. This prototype does not replace official SAPS systems, station procedures, legal review or evidence-management controls.

The legal and directive reference page plus compliance checkpoints are operational prompts only. Confirm current requirements with official legislation, SAPS instructions, NPA guidance and authorized legal advice.

## Run the workflow tests

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

The tests use a temporary SQLite database rather than the local project database.

## Run locally

```powershell
python -m venv .venv
.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
pip install -r requirements.txt
python app.py
```

Open [http://127.0.0.1:8000](http://127.0.0.1:8000).
